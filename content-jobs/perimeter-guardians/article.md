Follow these high-level steps — do not assume locations or numbers:
1. Approach or unlock the region boundary guarded by a perimeter guardian. The game ties those encounters to world progression.  
2. Defeat the perimeter guardian encountered at the boundary. After a guardian is defeated, the world map will expand to reveal additional area(s).  
3. Use the newly revealed map area to continue main-world progression and exploration (new routes, objectives, or content may be available after expansion).
