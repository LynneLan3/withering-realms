Quick answer
Arm talismans modify your limb attacks (they can boost damage or reduce resource use) and body talismans occupy four separate slots that provide protective effects or can retrigger arm talisman effects (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL). The Early Access build contains 70+ talismans and this page is a system overview, not a tier list (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL). Patch 0.36 (released 2026-09-09) contains named reclassifications for several The Freak talismans and a fix for magic-toll talismans such as Four Eyes occasionally miscounting (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL). Research checked 2026-09-10; Early Access Steam release: 2026-09-04 (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL).

# Withering Realms Talismans Guide

## Arm talismans — confirmed role only
- Role (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL): Arm talismans attach to limb attacks and modify those attacks' behaviour. In Early Access they are confirmed to either increase damage or reduce resource consumption for affected attacks (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL).

## Body talismans — the four special slots
- Role (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL): Body talismans use one of four dedicated body slots. They provide defensive or support-style effects and, in some cases, can retrigger arm talisman effects when their conditions are met (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL).
