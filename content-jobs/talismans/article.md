# Withering Realms Talismans Guide

## Arm talismans — what they do
- Role (confirmed): Arm talismans modify the behaviour of your limb attacks. In Early Access they are reported to either increase damage or reduce resource consumption for those attacks (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL).

## Four Arms + Birdcage — player reports (not an official bug)
- Player reports indicate that equipping the Four Arms body talisman may cause the Birdcage / B2 hanging-target puzzle to count an extra hit in its sequence. These reports are community-observed and remain PLAYER_REPORTED only. (PLAYER_REPORTED)  
- One player reported that the puzzle’s incorrect responses stopped after unequipping Four Arms (PLAYER_REPORTED). Do not treat this as an official bug report or confirmed fix.
