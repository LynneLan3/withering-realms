# Withering Realms Doll Arms & Limb Leveling

Quick answer
- The Doll uses a missing right-hand / right-arm tool system: you equip crafted right-arm tools that change the Doll’s attacks. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
- Crafted arm tools provide both a primary and a secondary attack action. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
- Official store examples of crafted arm tools include Enchanted Candelabrum, Chain Rock, Machinegun, and Flamethrower. The official catalog is incomplete. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
- Clover can cast while the Doll attacks. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
- The game’s progression and equipment explicitly support limb-oriented buildcrafting (i.e., building your character around specific Doll arms and limb choices). (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)

How the Doll arm system works — confirmed facts only
1. The Doll is presented with a missing right hand and a right-arm tool slot; equipped right-arm tools replace that limb’s functionality. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
2. When you craft and equip a right-arm tool, that tool supplies both a primary attack and a secondary attack for the Doll. These dual actions are part of the crafted-tool design rather than being generic weapon swaps. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
3. Doll progression and equipment are structured to support limb-oriented buildcrafting — players can center builds around which Doll arm/tool they run. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
4. Clover (the companion character) is able to cast while the Doll performs its attacks; casting and Doll-attacking are not mutually exclusive. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)
