How the Doll arm system works — confirmed facts only
1. The Doll is presented with a missing right hand and a right-arm tool slot; equipping a right-arm tool replaces that limb’s functionality. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
2. Crafted right-arm tools supply both a primary attack and a secondary attack — each crafted tool defines those two actions in its design. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
3. Doll progression and equipment are structured to support limb-oriented buildcrafting: players can center builds around which Doll arm/tool they run. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)  
4. Clover (the companion) can cast while the Doll performs its attacks; casting and Doll-attacking are not mutually exclusive. (CONFIRMED_OFFICIAL)
