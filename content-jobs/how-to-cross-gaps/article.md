# How to Cross Gaps in Withering Realms

Quick answer
Defeat the creature trapped by arms in the cemetery → pick up the chain reward dropped by that encounter → take the chain reward to the person who works on the Doll → they will create a traversal tool → use that traversal tool to cross the blocked gap. This progression is the currently player-confirmed route to open the blocked gap.

*MEDIA_SLOT:blocked-gap*

Step-by-step progression
1. Defeat the cemetery creature.
   - Complete the encounter against the creature trapped by arms in the cemetery. Players reporting the current progression identify this encounter as the source of the next item.
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:cemetery-creature*

2. Claim the chain reward.
   - After that fight, collect the chain reward awarded by the encounter. (Player reports indicate the item appears as a chain-type reward in the encounter reward screen.)
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:reward-tooltip*

3. Take the chain reward to the person who works on the Doll.
   - Deliver the chain reward to the person who works on the Doll (do not confuse with other NPCs). That person accepts the reward and performs work on the Doll.
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:doll-craftsman*

4. Receive the traversal tool.
   - The Doll worker creates a traversal tool from the chain reward. Players confirm this yields the traversal tool needed for the blocked gap.
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:finished-traversal-tool*

5. Use the traversal tool to cross the blocked gap.
   - With the traversal tool created, use it at the blocked gap to cross. Players confirm the traversal tool is the item used to open/cross that specific blocked gap.
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:crossing-result*

Naming note — why this page uses "chain reward" and "traversal tool"
- The item names and the tool’s official in-game labels are not yet verified. Steam players have reported a spelling shown as "icorised chain" (PLAYER_REPORTED spelling only), but the official in-game tooltip/name has not been confirmed. For clarity and to avoid inventing or mislabeling content, this guide uses the neutral terms "the chain reward" and "the traversal tool" until official names are confirmed. (Name spelling is NEEDS_VERIFICATION.)

If this didn't work
- Missing reward: If you do not have the chain reward after the cemetery encounter, verify that you completed the encounter; if it still does not appear, players report this as a possible progression-block and it may require an update or community confirmation.
- Cannot craft: If the Doll worker will not accept the chain reward or will not create the traversal tool despite having the reward, this is currently provisional—players have reported the crafting step as the required action, but behaviour may vary across builds.
- Still blocked: If you have the traversal tool but cannot cross the gap, confirm you delivered the chain reward to the Doll worker and received the traversal tool as described; if the tool exists but does not function, report details to official patch notes or support (see Verification block below).

FAQ
Q: Where exactly do I bring the chain reward?
A: Bring the chain reward to the person who works on the Doll. The guide uses that wording because the craftsman’s proper name was not supplied in the available reports.

Q: Is the chain called "icorised chain"?
A: That exact spelling was observed on Steam by players and is a PLAYER_REPORTED spelling only. The official in-game tooltip or name has not been verified (NEEDS_VERIFICATION).

Q: What does the traversal tool do?
A: Players confirm the traversal tool created by the Doll worker is used to cross the blocked gap described above. This is the current confirmed progression for early traversal in the game.
