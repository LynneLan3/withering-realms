# How to Cross Gaps in Withering Realms

Quick answer  
Defeat the Chain Giant in the cemetery → pick up the Ichorised Chain reward → take the Ichorised Chain to the Doll craftsman Rakesh → obtain the Chain Hook (the game’s grappling-hook tool) for £1 → use the Chain Hook to cast into wooden pilings or enemies to cross pits. This is the current Early Access progression from reward to traversal tool.

*MEDIA_SLOT:blocked-gap*

Step-by-step progression
1. Defeat the Chain Giant.  
   - The cemetery encounter shows the enemy name on the HUD as "Chain Giant."  
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:cemetery-creature*

2. Claim the Ichorised Chain reward.  
   - After the fight the encounter reward popup reads exactly: "You found... Ichorised Chain" (a Crafting Material). The on-screen tooltip description: "A rare length of chain unearthed deep within a mine. Having been saturated in ichor for many years, it is remarkably strong and light." / "In the right hands, several powerful tools could be fashioned from it."  
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:reward-tooltip*

3. Bring the Ichorised Chain to Rakesh (the Doll craftsman).  
   - The NPC is identified on the dialogue/UI as Rakesh; dialogue shown: "So, you have found a length of Ichorised Chain." Deliver the chain to him at his workbench.  
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:doll-craftsman*

4. Receive the Chain Hook (Doll Left Hand).  
   - Rakesh provides the finished traversal tool named "Chain Hook" for £1. The Chain Hook tooltip text on-screen reads: "A length of ichorised chain tipped with a weighted dagger. Can be cast into wooden pilings to cross pits, or into enemies to close the distance quickly." Controls and stats shown in the same UI: "+Left Trigger Throw hook (Dexterity/Precision)"; 5 physical | 15 poise; 8 stamina toll.  
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:finished-traversal-tool*

5. Use the Chain Hook at blocked gaps / pits.  
   - The Chain Hook’s on-screen description states it can be cast into wooden pilings to cross pits. The preservation of the progression chain is: defeat cemetery creature → claim Ichorised Chain → take to Rakesh → receive Chain Hook → use tool to cross blocked gaps.  
   - *MEDIA_SLOT:crossing-result* (MISSING) — a direct in-VOD clip showing the player actually swinging across the gap was not captured in the available footage; the tool’s tooltip is the source for its pit-crossing function.

Naming note — mapping "grappling hook" searches to the official name  
- Public search queries for a "grappling hook" or "hook shot" should be mapped to the official in-game tool name: Chain Hook. The streamer used vernacular like "hook shot" or "grappling-hook-like tool," but the verified, on-screen name is Chain Hook and the chain item is spelled Ichorised Chain. Do not treat "grappling hook" as a separate official item name.

If this didn't work
- Missing reward: If the Ichorised Chain does not appear after the cemetery fight, confirm the encounter completed. If still missing, this has been reported as a possible progression issue across builds and may need further verification from patch notes or support.  
- Cannot trade/craft: If Rakesh will not accept the chain or will not provide the Chain Hook despite having Ichorised Chain, ensure you spoke to the correct Doll craftsman (Rakesh) and that you have the chain in your inventory. Behavior may vary by build.  
- Tool exists but gap still blocked: The Chain Hook’s tooltip states it crosses pits, but a captured in-game clip of the crossing result is not present in the current footage. If you have the Chain Hook and cannot progress, record steps and report to official support or check later patch notes.
