# Withering Realms Bellflower Institute Stuck on First Floor

Quick answer
If you hit a gap or a corridor door with glowing “ghost-hands” (Open (A) prompt) and can’t go forward, turn around and go back to the foyer. At the rear of the foyer, next to the corridor/foyer transition, there is an unmarked vertical exit — a wooden rear stair / climb spot that shows Climb Up (A) / Climb Down (A). Use that climb/stairs to leave the blocked corridor. The climb/stair is not shown as a labeled icon on the in‑game mini‑map. This page covers only this stuck first‑floor situation (not the separate Institute Code Words / basement puzzles).

What the stuck state looks like
- Common presentation: an apparent dead‑end in the first‑floor corridor with either a visible gap in the floor/route or a door that displays glowing hands and an Open (A) prompt.

Exact steps to leave the blocked corridor and use the unmarked rear stairs
Follow these steps in order:

1. Stop trying to force the blocked door or push past the gap. Turn and walk back the way you came down the corridor toward the foyer.
2. Enter the foyer and move to the rear edge of that room where the corridor meets the foyer (the area shown in the footage as the foyer/corridor transition).
3. Look for a vertical transit prompt at the rear of the foyer — the game shows Climb Up (A) or Climb Down (A) when you’re in the correct spot.  
*MEDIA_SLOT:climb-up-exit*  
_Proof: Climb Up (A) prompt visible at the foyer rear; location banner present in footage._
4. Use the Climb Up (A) prompt (or Climb Down if you need to go lower) to take the wooden rear stairs / climb exit. The wooden rear stairs are visually present at the foyer rear; the mini‑map does not show a stair icon for this exit.  
*MEDIA_SLOT:foyer-rear-stairs*  
_Proof: Wooden rear stairs at foyer rear; mini‑map in footage shows the foyer without a stair icon._

Common failure modes
- Searching farther forward down the blocked corridor instead of backtracking — players repeatedly try to find a forward path and miss the actual exit behind them.  
- Trusting the mini‑map to show every climb/stair — this rear vertical exit does not appear as a labeled stair icon on the in‑game mini‑map in observed footage.

What happens next
- After using the rear climb/stairs, the location banner in observed Early Access footage changes to Bellflower Occult Institute - Upper (when ascending). The vertical exit can be used both up and down (Climb Up / Climb Down prompts were visible in the footage).  
*MEDIA_SLOT:upper-progression*  
_Proof: location banner Bellflower Occult Institute - Upper after ascending._
- Using the rear exit moves you out of the blocked first‑floor corridor and continues normal progression from the foyer vertical transit.

Troubleshooting
- I don’t see a Climb Up/Down prompt: move closer to the rear wall of the foyer where the corridor meets the room. The prompt only appears when you’re in the small climb/stairs interaction zone.  
- Mini‑map shows the foyer but no stair icon: this is expected — the stair/climb exit is not marked on the in‑game mini‑map in observed footage. Do not rely on a map icon to find it.
- If you still can’t find it, backtrack fully to the foyer entrance and sweep the rear edge of the room where the corridor connects; the climb prompt is near that transition.

FAQ
Q: Why doesn’t the mini‑map show the rear stairs or climb?  
A: In Early Access footage the mini‑map displays the foyer geometry but does not mark this climb/stair as a labeled stair icon. The climb is visible in the world and appears as a Climb Up (A) / Climb Down (A) prompt when you stand in the interaction zone.

Q: Is this the Institute Code Words / basement door puzzle?  
A: No. The blocked corridor (gap / ghost‑hands door) that requires returning to the foyer rear climb is a separate issue. The Institute Code Words and basement‑door puzzles are distinct and are covered by their own guides.
